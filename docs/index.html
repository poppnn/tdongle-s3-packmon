No install, no toolchain — plug the dongle in and click.

How to flash

  1. Hold the BOOT button on the dongle, plug it into a USB port, then release.
  2. Click Install poppn packmon above.
  3. Pick the serial port in the browser dialog (USB JTAG/serial debug unit or a COM/ttyACM port).
  4. Choose Install, confirm the erase, and wait ~30 s.
  5. Unplug and replug. The splash animation means you're done.

What it does

  • Promiscuous 802.11 sniffing across channels 1–13, hopping every 500 ms.
  • Five pages you cycle with the dongle's button — no config, no app.
  • Live — packets per second, running totals and a 30 s rate graph.
  • Channels — activity bars for all 13 channels, busiest one called out.
  • Networks — nearby APs with SSID, channel, encryption and signal.
  • Threats — deauth log with attacker MAC, channel, RSSI and age.
  • System — uptime, frame-type breakdown, free RAM and frame rate.
  • Deauth / disassoc detection interrupts any page with a full-screen alert.
  • Everything renders through a framebuffer at 30 fps — no flicker, animated throughout.
  • APA102 status LED shifts colour with traffic load, strobes red on attack.

Legal

Receive-only. packmon listens; it never transmits deauth frames or any other attack traffic. Passively monitoring radio spectrum is still regulated in some jurisdictions — check your local law before running it, and only monitor networks you own or are authorised to test.
